# Data protection notice (ratings study)

**Purpose.** Research on online ratings and local competition in the French hearing-aid market (working paper *Ratings without exit*, 2026).

**Sources.** (1) RPPS public extraction (Agence du Numérique en Santé, file *Personne_activite*, August 2026, Licence Ouverte 2.0), which lists registered health professionals with their professional addresses; (2) Google Places API (New), official access, collected 3 September 2026; (3) publicly accessible provider websites, read 3–4 September 2026 in compliance with robots.txt; (4) commune-level statistics from the companion accessibility study.

**Processing.** Practitioner names were used only to classify sites by brand (legal-name mapping) and were not otherwise processed. Google ratings were linked to sites in a working file held by the author for the analyses of the paper.

**What is published.** `sites_public_v2.csv`: one row per site with the RPPS structure identifier, commune code, organisational form, brand (chains and networks only), owner-practitioner flag, number of practitioners top-coded at 4, competition measures, sampling stratum and weight, and coded website variables. No practitioner name, registration number, mode of practice or street address; no Google field.

**What is not published.** Site-level Google data (rating, review count, website address, place identifier), raw website text, the trade and legal names of independent centres.

**Legal basis.** Legitimate interest for research purposes (GDPR, Article 6(1)(f)) with the safeguards of Article 89. Retention of the working file: until the final version of the paper is published, for replication requests answered by re-analysis only.

**Rights and contact.** Persons whose data appear in the register may contact the author (nsoussan0@gmail.com) to exercise their rights of access and objection regarding this processing.
